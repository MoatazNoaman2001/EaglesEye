EaglesEye — Logo Concepts, Round 2

Your brief: an eagle head — but the eye is robotic. The bird brings power and instinct; the machine eye brings the platform: cameras, GPS, precision. Sky-blue palette throughout, neck feathers kept as the feather element. Three treatments below, from aggressive-tech to badge to minimal.

Variant A

Cyber Raptor — angular

EaglesEye

Sharp geometry, targeting-reticle eye. The head is faceted like cut stone — aggressive and unmistakably tech. The eye is a glowing HUD reticle with crosshair ticks: lock-on, tracking, precision. The jagged lower edge is the feather reference. Strongest personality of the three.

Variant B

Aperture Eagle — badge

EaglesEye

White eagle head on a sky disc, camera-lens eye. The eye is a glass lens with aperture blades — a dashcam / camera reference, friendlier than a reticle. Badge shape drops perfectly into app icons, vehicle stickers and favicons. The most "trustworthy SaaS" of the three.

Variant C

Line Sentinel — minimal

EaglesEye

One continuous line, one glowing eye. The head is drawn in a single confident outline; all the colour lives in the robotic reticle eye. Cleanest on documents, invoices and the console header; scales to a filled silhouette at tiny sizes. The most corporate/minimal treatment.
