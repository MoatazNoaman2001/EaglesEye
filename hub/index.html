What We're Building — Phase 1 Modules

Every card below is a service or feature of the EaglesEye platform, traced to the BRD.

Multi-Protocol Ingestion

One platform, any GPS hardware. Pluggable decoders for Teltonika Codec 8/8E, Concox/GT06, a third family, and generic MQTT/JSON — with guaranteed no-loss acknowledgement.

FR-ING2,000 msg/splugin SPI

Live Tracking Console

Every vehicle on one map, updating in real time. Status at a glance — moving, idle, stopped, offline — with speed, ignition, voltage and street address per vehicle.

FR-TRK5s latencyMapLibre

Trips & History

Raw GPS becomes trustworthy trips: automatic segmentation, road-snapped paths, distance you can run payroll on, and full history replay with playback controls.

FR-TRPmap matching2% odometer

Geofencing

Draw zones on the map — sites, depots, restricted areas. Get precise entry/exit events, dwell times, and after-hours rules per vehicle or group.

FR-GEOPostGIStime windows

Rules & Alerts

Ten operational event types with per-fleet thresholds and time windows. Deduplication and cooldowns are built-in — alerts stay meaningful, never noisy.

FR-ALT10 event typesanti-noise

Device Health & Diagnostics

The support-cost killer. Every device's signal, GPS fix, voltage and firmware — with automatic classification of why a unit went quiet, before anyone drives to site.

FR-HLT80% remote diagnosiscross-tenant

Reports & Exports

Trips, utilisation, stops, speeding, geofence visits, inactivity — over any date range, exported to Excel and Arabic-correct PDF, or scheduled straight to your inbox.

FR-RPTExcel + PDFscheduled

Multi-Tenancy & Access Control

Many fleets, one platform, zero data leakage — isolation enforced in the database itself. Roles from Platform Admin to Installer, group-level visibility, full audit trail.

FR-SECRLSreseller-ready

Public API & Webhooks

Your data is yours. A documented REST API with scoped tokens, plus webhooks that push alerts and geofence events straight into your ERP or ops tools — with retry logic.

FR-APIOpenAPIERP-ready

Arabic-First, Bilingual

Full Arabic and English interfaces with proper RTL layout — including exported PDFs. Built for regional teams, with tenant-local timezones and metric units throughout.
